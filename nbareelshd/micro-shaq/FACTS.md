# Facts (checked 2026-11-28)
- 19 NBA seasons; 1 three-pointer made in 22 attempts (4.5 %), 16 Feb 1996, Orlando vs Milwaukee (121-91), at a buzzer. — Fox Sports, Orlando Pinstriped Post, Sportskeeda.
- Career free throws 52.7 %; 5,317 missed, second-most in NBA history (Wilt Chamberlain 5,805). — Grunge, Sportskeeda.
- "Hack-a-Shaq": fouling him on purpose; the name comes from its use against him (1999). — Wikipedia.
- 4 NBA titles (2000, 2001, 2002, 2006).
