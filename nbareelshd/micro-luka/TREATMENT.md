# Micro Luka: "Nobody saw it coming" (Microgame Frenzy, vertical)

1080×1920, 24 fps, ~32 s, Spanish, the user's cloned narrator voice. No logos, no team marks: the hero is a generic player whose marks survive every medium — **the number 77 in electric blue** and a **headband**.

## Three candidate structures
1. **A relay**: each game's result is handed to the next. Rejected: Luka's story is dates, not objects.
2. **Two players alternating** (Luka vs "Dallas"). Rejected: it explains the trade too late.
3. **Start inside the last game, then rewind the career** (chosen): we open mid-game with the trade (the hook and the controversy formula yt-viral found), the stage rewinds to his debut, the games speed up through his records, a funny failure (he cannot pass Wilt and Kobe), and the trade game returns to close the loop.

## Logline and arc
The trade nobody saw coming → how good he was (5 games, ever faster) → the one thing he could not do → the trade again, now absurd → "¿el traspaso más loco?"

## Home stage
Violet ground with a rotating sunburst, a TV on a pedestal (the frame-within-the-frame), a host: a basketball mascot with eyes that ticks on every beat. Lives: 3 basketballs. Badge: STAGE n. Success = lime ✓, fail = red ✗.

## Benchmark
Learn the party-game grammar (command → action → stamp, lives, speed-up) — never its name, characters, UI or music.

## Cue map / sound
Groove: kit + slap-ish bass, 118 bpm, 136 after SPEED UP. One colour instrument per medium: square beeps (terminal), toy piano (crayon), vibes (riso), pulse arpeggio (pixel), typewriter (blueprint), claves (Swiss). Foley per medium. Stab + whistle on every command; glass crack on the lost life; real silence (lights cut) before the final stab, the loudest hit.

## Retention (yt-retention rules)
Frame 1 is already a game with the command slamming. A medium switch every 3–4 s (no cliff without an event). 32 s total.
